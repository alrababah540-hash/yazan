# Research references and data sources

Research date: 23 Nov 2026. The official sites (adrec.gov.ae, mediaoffice.abudhabi, zawya.com) were blocked from this production environment's network. Every figure was therefore cross-checked across at least two independent outlets that quote the same official release. Figures that could not be confirmed that way were left out (see the bottom of this file).

## Deliberately omitted (could not be confirmed to the required standard)
- **Rental yields**: the only Q3 2026 figure found ("up to 11.5%") is an asking-price estimate for selected communities (Bayut), not a market measure.
- **2024 transaction value**: not confirmed directly, so no 2024 bar is drawn.
- **Share of foreign buyers in 2025**: found only in secondary summaries.
- **Infrastructure timelines** (rail, road) and **forecasts**: no official dated figures were verified. The Outlook panel lists announced projects only and is labelled "not forecasts".
- ValuStrat's +24% apartment growth (Q3 2026): its methodology differs from Cavendish Maxwell's. Only one consultancy's series is shown, so like is compared with like.
