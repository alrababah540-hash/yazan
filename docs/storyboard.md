# Storyboard & shot list — "From an empty plot to a real-estate decision"

Format 1080×1920 · 30 fps · 76.0 s (2,280 frames) · one continuous 3D world (city → map → studio → city → stone table).
Emotional arc: **Curiosity → Understanding → Confidence → (Caution) → Informed action**.
Design system: midnight navy `#0B1626`, charcoal `#23262B`, ivory `#F3EEE4`, natural stone `#D6C9B1`, champagne gold `#C9A96A`, muted emerald `#2F6B57`. Type: IBM Plex Sans Arabic (300–700) + Cormorant Garamond for the Latin "OFF-PLAN" wordmark.

Thumbnails of every shot are in `docs/stills/` (rendered from the final scene).

| Scene | Time | Shot / camera | Visual action | Transition out (reason) | Psychology |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
